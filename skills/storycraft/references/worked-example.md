# Worked example: "The 3 A.M. Reading" (Architect mode, short film ~14 minutes)

A compact demonstration of the full Architect workflow on an original story. Use it to calibrate depth and format,
not as a formula: a different story would need different choices.

**Brief (as a user might give it):** "A sad, mysterious short film, ~15 minutes, small cast, one location. I want an
ending that hits hard and makes sense on a second watch."
**Assumptions stated:** live-action, two main characters, a remote setting that supports isolation and a clock.

---

## 1. Story core
**Logline:** A young technician sent to automate a remote mountain weather station must certify its 22-year record by
dawn, but the station's last human observer has left one reading blank every winter, and tonight is that night.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who wants what, why now? | **Ari** (28) must certify the station's continuous record by 06:00, when the automated system takes over; his job (and a climate study that relies on the record) depends on it. |
| Opposition | **Tove** (58), the observer, won't explain the gaps; a blizzard; Ari's own impatience. Inner: Ari believes data is truer than people. |
| Stakes | External: the record is thrown out and Ari's first assignment fails. Internal: Ari's belief that facts matter more than feelings. Philosophical: is a record worth more than the person who keeps it? |
| Tove's wound | Twenty-two years ago she insisted her husband **Lars** take the 03:00 manual reading in a storm ("the series must never break"); he never came back. |
| Dramatic question (main tension) | Will Ari find out why the 03:00 readings are missing before dawn? |
| Culmination | He learns the truth at ~02:40, which creates the final tension: will anyone go out at 03:00 in this storm? |
| Crisis (true dilemma) | Tove: honor the duty that killed Lars by completing the record, or keep the gap that is her only memorial to him. Ari: certify the record falsely to spare her, or report it truthfully. |
| Climax | Tove goes out at 03:00 for the first time in 22 years. Ari, against protocol and his own fear, follows her rope line; together they take the reading. |
| Resolution / key image | Dawn. The logbook's 03:00 line, written in Tove's hand, ends with two observer initials: "T. / L." Ari certifies the record and adds a note explaining the 21 earlier gaps as "observer's leave." |
| Emotional destination | Grief turned into an act of love; a young man learns that a record is a human promise. |
| Thematic argument | A promise kept out of love heals; a rule kept out of fear destroys. |
| Unity sentence | Because Lars died taking the 03:00 reading, Tove had to face that reading again before she could leave. |

## 2. Ending first
- **Crisis staged as a static, delayed moment:** 02:58, Tove stands at the door in her coat; the wind screams; the
  clock ticks; she can't open it. Hold it.
- **Turn inside the climax:** Ari expects to have to stop her, but instead she asks him to come. The gap between his
  expectation and the result flips his role from auditor to companion.
- **Inevitable but unexpected:** the planted rope line, Lars's coat on the hook, and Tove's ritual of setting two mugs
  make her choice feel inevitable in hindsight, yet the audience expected her either to refuse or to go alone.

## 3. Hidden story and information plan
**True chronology:** 22 winters ago, during a record storm, Lars wanted to skip the 03:00 reading. Tove, proud of the
station's unbroken series, insisted. He clipped onto the rope line, but the line was frayed (she had noticed it that
afternoon and meant to replace it). He was found in the spring. Every year since, on that date, she sets out two mugs,
leaves the 03:00 line blank, and writes "instrument fault." She has never told anyone about the rope.

| Secret | Audience learns | Ari learns | Mode created |
|---|---|---|---|
| The gaps fall on the same date every year | 3:00 (Ari's spreadsheet) | same moment | suspense (shared discovery) |
| Tonight is that date | 4:30 (calendar circled, two mugs) | 9:00 | **dramatic irony** → dread as Ari pushes her |
| Lars died taking the 03:00 reading | 9:00 (newspaper clipping in the logbook) | 9:00 | recognition (reversal of Ari's view of Tove) |
| The rope was frayed and she knew | 11:00 (she tells him at the door) | 11:00 | final recognition; turns grief into guilt and the choice into atonement |

Recognition owed: the audience knows the date before Ari, so his discovery scene (9:00) is mandatory.

## 4. Causal spine
| # | Beat | Link | Value turn | Audience hopes / fears |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ari arrives in the storm with the automated sensor; Tove barely greets him. | inciting cause | duty − | Will this cold woman cooperate? |
| 2 | Running the record, Ari finds a missing 03:00 reading every winter. | therefore | record intact + → − | Is she lazy? lying? |
| 3 | He asks; she says "instrument fault." He checks the instrument logs: no faults. | but | trust − | Why is she lying? |
| 4 | He presses: he must certify by 06:00. She refuses to discuss it and sets out two mugs. | therefore | conflict ↑ | (Irony) The audience sees the circled date; Ari doesn't. |
| 5 | The automatic sensor freezes; tonight's 03:00 reading can only be taken by hand. | but | clock ↑ | Someone will have to go out. |
| 6 | Ari decides he'll take it himself to protect the record; Tove forbids it, furious. | therefore | danger ↑ | Why does she care so much? |
| 7 | Searching the logbook for older calibration notes, Ari finds a clipping: Lars, lost taking the 03:00 reading, that date. | but | Ari's view of Tove − → + (compassion) | Recognition. Now: will she let anyone go out? |
| 8 | He offers to falsify tonight's entry so she can leave the gap one last time. | therefore | integrity − / kindness + | Will she accept? |
| 9 | She tells him about the frayed rope: she sent Lars out and knew. | but | grief → guilt | The real wound is revealed. |
| 10 | 02:58 at the door: she can't open it. | therefore | crisis | Will she go? |
| 11 | She asks Ari to come. They follow the new rope line together and take the reading. | therefore | atonement + | Will they make it back? |
| 12 | Dawn: the line "T. / L."; Ari certifies, with his note. | resolution | record + / love + | Bittersweet release. |

Escalation: Ari's attempts go from asking (3), to pressure (4), to doing it himself (6), to deception (8), until
only the either/or remains: go out together, or break the promise again.

## 5. Plants, clues, forward pull
| Plant | First shown | Payoff (new meaning) |
|---|---|---|
| Two mugs set out | beat 4 | She was setting a place for Lars every year. |
| Lars's coat on a hook, never moved | beat 1 (background) | Ari wears it to follow her at 03:00 (beat 11). |
| A new rope coil on the shelf, still in plastic | beat 1 | She replaced the rope every winter but never used it; tonight it's the line they follow. |
| Calendar date circled | beat 4 | The anniversary. |
| Ari's line "Data doesn't lie, people do" | beat 3 | Reversed at the end in his certification note. |
Advertising: "06:00 handover." Elements of the future: the storm forecast to peak at 03:00; Ari's promise to his
boss.

## 6. Emotional design
- Attachment: Tove's competence (reading the sky without instruments) and one dry joke; Ari's earnestness and fear of
  failing his first assignment.
- Preparation by contrast: a warm moment (beat 8, sharing tea from the second mug) just before the rope confession.
- Aftermath: dawn, silence, the logbook; no explanation of feelings.
- Subtext: their fight about "the record" (beats 3–6) is really a fight about guilt and grief.

## 7. Quality gate (excerpt)
- Causality: every beat linked by therefore/but ✔. Agency: Tove's choice decides the climax ✔. Fairness: date, mugs,
  coat, rope and clipping are all planted before the reveals ✔. Recognition owed and delivered ✔. Ending:
  inevitable (planted), unexpected (she asks him to come), the key image is a payoff ✔. Form: one location, two
  actors, 12 beats ≈ 14 minutes ✔.
