My short story feels flat and I can't figure out why. Can you tell me what's wrong and fix it? Here's the outline:
1. Maya is a nurse who works night shifts.
2. One night a mysterious old man is admitted with no ID.
3. Maya talks to him and he tells her about his life.
4. Then a storm knocks out the power.
5. Then Maya finds an old photo in his coat - it's her mother as a young woman.
6. Then the old man dies.
7. Then Maya finds a letter in the hospital lost-and-found that says he was her father who left before she was born.
8. She cries, goes home and calls her sister. The end.
