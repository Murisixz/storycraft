I'm planning a murder mystery novel (around 85k words) set at a remote lighthouse inn on the Scottish coast during a three-day storm. Eight guests, and on the first night the owner, Agnes, is found dead at the bottom of the lighthouse stairs. I want it to be fair-play so a sharp reader could solve it, and I want the killer's reason to be heartbreaking rather than greedy. Help me design the plot: what really happened, the suspects, the clues and red herrings, and the order in which things get revealed.
