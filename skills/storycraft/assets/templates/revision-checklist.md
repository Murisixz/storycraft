# Revision Checklist: [Title / draft #]

Run the passes in order, one question per pass. Full method: `references/revision.md`. Record evidence (page,
scene, count) for every "no".

## 0. Before changing anything
- [ ] Strengths listed (what must survive):
- [ ] Intent stated (the emotional destination and core idea):
- [ ] If responding to feedback: each claim tested against the text (claim | evidence | verdict | cause | fix).

## 1. Scope and shape
- [ ] The material (time span, arcs, reveals, world) fits the length (`scope-budget`).
- [ ] Every decisive choice, reversal, confrontation, recognition and consequence is on the must-stage list.

## 2. Spine, agency, consequence
- [ ] Every beat links by therefore/but, and the protagonist makes the decisive choices.
- [ ] Each major event leaves a later scene in which someone acts differently because of it.
- [ ] Reported climaxes are either staged or made dramatic as tellings (the teller wants something; the listener resists).

## 3. Information and inference
- [ ] Leak audit done: the inference point is recorded for each secret.
- [ ] The inference point comes close enough to the reveal for the design (mystery), or the secret is used as deliberate irony.
- [ ] Each secret has one strategy (mystery, suspense or irony) per stretch; no mixed signals.
- [ ] At least one rival explanation is alive until near the reveal (for mystery-led designs).
- [ ] The reveal recontextualizes earlier scenes, or its payload is a consequence or decision.

## 4. Scene and summary
- [ ] Staged / reported / summarized is marked for each key event, with reasons for every non-scene.
- [ ] Long spans are anchored by staged instances.
- [ ] The scene share rises where the story turns.

## 5. People
- [ ] Each major character has a life beyond the plot, shown in at least one action.
- [ ] Allies and kind characters show self-interest or friction at least once.
- [ ] Secret-keepers get their own side: pressure, justification, cost.
- [ ] Voice test passed (speakers distinguishable without tags).
- [ ] Each major character has one surprising but true action.

## 6. World
- [ ] Channel audit done: every obvious workaround is closed with a visible reason.
- [ ] Enforcement is shown at least once; daily life under the rules is felt.
- [ ] The abstraction level (specific / invented / fable) is chosen and consistent.

## 7. Emotion, ending, proportion
- [ ] Cost ledger: who loses what; who keeps paying.
- [ ] The settlement is proportionate to the loss or deception (time, cost, partialness).
- [ ] Tidiness tally: something honest stays open.
- [ ] At least one consequence scene after the main revelation shows a costly choice.

## 8. Prose and visible machinery
- [ ] Motif inventory: only the load-bearing echoes kept.
- [ ] Symmetries broken except the one that matters.
- [ ] Comparisons and number words per 1,000 words checked against the benchmarks; no dominant construction.
- [ ] Heightened register reserved for the peaks.

## 9. Continuity
- [ ] Master timeline checked: dates, ages, counts, names, travel times, who knows what when.
