**Conventions**
- **Narration:**
  - one breath per row;
  - the role before each new name;
  - numbers written as the narrator will say them;
  - the news at the end of the sentence.

  Mark a held pause inside a line as `{pause 2 s}`. Never use square brackets in the narration column: Eleven v4 reads
  anything in square brackets as an audio tag, so production marks must stay out of the text sent to a voice model
  (`references/documentary.md` §20.4).
- **Picture:** what is shown. Mark every reconstruction, painting, generated image or dramatised clip as **RECON**, **ART**, **GEN** or **DRAMA**, with how it is labelled on screen. Give each strand its own look, so that the viewer can tell the strand as well as the medium.
- **Sound and pause:** music in and out, effects, archival audio, and the length of each pause the viewer needs.
- **Ledger:** the status and row in the documentary plan's truth ledger, one status per row. An **F** row needs its frame or seam marker in the narration, or within the frame the viewer was given. An **A** row (the adaptation's own addition) is voiced as the video's own, never "in the novel". A row that mixes statuses needs its seam marked inside the sentence.
- **Other voices:** name the speaker before the row (VOICE: the inquiry report; VOICE: a diver's son, recorded 1975), and end with a row in which the narrator takes the telling back.

**Before handing it on**
- [ ] Read aloud at the narrator's pace and times corrected; or, if you cannot read it aloud, times marked as an estimate, per language, with no line needing an impossible pace.
- [ ] The narration alone carries every fact the story stands on (the audio-only test).
- [ ] Every quotation ends audibly; every claim's status is heard with it; every F line is marked.
- [ ] No row points at a picture without saying what it shows.
- [ ] Cold listen logged (`comprehension-log.md`, "Cold listen" table).
