## Fair-play check
- [ ] Every link in the solution chain is supported by a clue shown before the solution.
- [ ] The culprit appears early and has real presence.
- [ ] No solution-critical clue is withheld from the audience.
- [ ] No accident or unexplained intuition solves the case.
- [ ] Every red herring has an innocent explanation that is revealed.
- [ ] Most discoveries are earned (observation, inference, risk, mistake, extracted testimony), not volunteered.
- [ ] Each false lead is believed by the reader, changes an action, and costs something before it dies.
- [ ] No suspect carries three or more early markers unless dramatic irony is the chosen design.
- [ ] Every plot-carrying mechanism passes the expert's objections.
