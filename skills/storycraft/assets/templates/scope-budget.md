## 3. Fit decision
- [ ] **Fits:** the must-stage scenes plus connective summary fit the target.
- [ ] **Too much material.** Choose one:
  - [ ] **Narrow** the window, cut arcs or merge characters (list what goes): ____
  - [ ] **Re-time** with a frame, braided timelines or a retrospective selection (describe): ____
  - [ ] **Expand** to the form the material needs (novella / novel / series): ____
- [ ] **Too little material** for the length: deepen (a subplot that varies the theme, a second line of action, a
  stronger opposition) rather than stretching scenes.
