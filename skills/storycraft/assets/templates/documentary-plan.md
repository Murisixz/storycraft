## 2. Truth ledger
H = established; C = claimed by a named source; U = uncertain, disputed or unverified; F = the novel's invention (F-claim: a
character's account; F-open: left open by the novel); A = added by the adaptation itself (voiced as the video's own,
never "in the novel"). One status per sentence: a sentence that mixes statuses is a seam and needs a marker. World
details inside invented scenes (gear, routines, how debts were kept) are H rows too. Keep each source's modality
("expected to", "frequently"). If most H rows rest on one source, name it aloud once, with its kind.
