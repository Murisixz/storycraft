# Comprehension Log (cold read): [Title / edition / draft #]

Method: `references/clarity-and-orientation.md` §12. Give the reader a copy **without** the "Writer only" section.
The reader works from the text alone: no outline, no plan, and no other-language edition. Log while reading, and fill
"Resolved at" as you go.

## Log
| P# | Orientation before (where/when; who is present; who last acted; what is pursued) | Type | Exact words | Best guess (confidence) | Severity (minor / moderate / serious) | Resolved at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Types: referent · speaker · thing · name-or-word · homograph · place-time · action · link · unestablished · mentioned-word or mechanism · dialect or foreign · register or collocation · layout

Severity:
- minor: a pause, resolved within the sentence;
- moderate: a reread, or a wrong guess carried for a paragraph or more;
- serious: a wrong picture of a plot fact that persists, or the reader stops building the model.

## Turning points (reader)
At each moment where something irreversible may have happened, write what you believe happened and how sure you are.
Do not look ahead.
| At P# | What I believe just happened | Confidence | What told me (a line, an act, a ritual, a reaction, a later mention) |
|---|---|---|---|

## Cold listen (narration heard once: documentaries, narrated video, audio)
The listener hears, or reads, only the narration track, in order, once, with no plan and no pictures
(`references/documentary.md` §17). At the end of each section:

| After section | What I believe happened | Who is who (and how I know) | Established / claimed / uncertain / invented: what I heard as which | What is still open | Where I lost the thread (time or line) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
